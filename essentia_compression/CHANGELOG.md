# Changelog

All notable changes to this package are documented in this file. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the package adheres to
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## 1.0.0

### Added

- `package:essentia_compression/essentia_compression.dart`.
- `gzip`, `gunzip`, `gzipEncoder` and `gzipDecoder`: one gzip member of RFC 1952, whose header,
  CRC-32 and length decoding checks, on the zlib of `dart:io` and the `CompressionStream` of the
  browser.
- `deflate`, `inflate`, `deflateEncoder` and `deflateDecoder`: zlib streams of RFC 1950, whose
  header and Adler-32 decoding checks, and with `raw` deflate data alone.
- `unbrotli` and `brotliDecoder`: a decoder of Brotli, RFC 7932, written in Dart and the same on
  every platform, that decodes a stream as it arrives and refuses damaged, cut short and trailing
  data; the static dictionary is generated by `tool/brotli/generate_dictionary.dart`.
- `Crc32` and `Adler32`, the running checks of gzip and zlib.
